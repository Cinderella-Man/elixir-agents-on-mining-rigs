Cancelled departures in the v2 audio departures view are read out wrong: the row has time: nil, so it isn't spoken as cancelled. Read the first cancelled departure in a group as "The, Needham Line, train to, South Station; scheduled for, 12:05, is cancelled" and later ones with "The following," instead of "The,". If scheduled_time is nil too, leave out the scheduled part, e.g. "The following, Needham Line, train to, South Station; is cancelled".
