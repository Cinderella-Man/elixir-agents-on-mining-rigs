### Agentic RL and a DeepSeek teacher

Every idea from the 2026-11-03 research on FireFlash's agentic RL recipe and DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash-1017 as a teacher has a
row here ([report](docs/research/finetuning/reports/Agentic%20RL%20for%20a%20DeepSeek%20teacher.md)).
The verdict: use DeepSeek as it is, through its own API (MIT weights; its terms allow distillation), as a test-filtered
data source; put RL on the student; train the teacher only for an Elixir tail it cannot reach with 50 attempts and hints.
